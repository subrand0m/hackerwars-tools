Research progression table
From v— upward (at or above NPC max). Each row is one whole-version research target.
What do these columns mean?
Target
Version you're researching up to. Each row is one whole-version step.
+ $/hr
Earnings gain over the row above (or over the effective base for the first row). Cumulative — v25.0's gain stacks on top of v24.0's, not on top of the NPC-base row.
Single-tick cost
Cost to research just this one version, with no batching surcharge. You'd queue it on its own, let it finish, then start the next.
Batched cost
Cost to research from your current version up to this one in a single queued batch. Includes the batching surcharge — bigger batches pay more per version.
Payback (single)
How long the new + $/hr takes to repay the single-tick cost.
Payback (batch)
How long the new + $/hr takes to repay the batched cost. You only realize the gain once the whole batch finishes.

Sub-PC purchase ladder
Each fully-kitted sub-PC adds $0/hr at your current spam settings. Fully kitted = the CPU tier you picked (i9 13900k 6 GHz / $15,000) + the RAM tier you picked (Deadly DDR4 4 GB / $5,000) + SSD2 2 GB HDD ($1,000, baked in) → kit total $21,000. Server price climbs through #9, then floors at $1,000,000 for #10+.
What do these columns mean?
#
Which sub-PC this would be in your cluster. #2 is your second sub-PC (your localhost is #1 and isn't shown here).
Server
Base server price for this slot. Climbs through #9, then floors at $1,000,000 from #10 onward.
+ Kit
Added cost to fully kit this sub-PC: the CPU tier you picked + the RAM tier you picked + SSD2 2 GB HDD ($1,000). Same per sub-PC regardless of slot.
Marginal
Total cost to add just this one sub-PC (Server + Kit). What you actually pay if you stop here.
Cumulative
All-in cost of every sub-PC from #2 through this row, summed.
Payback (this PC)
How long this sub-PC's added $/hr takes to repay its own marginal cost — not the cumulative.
